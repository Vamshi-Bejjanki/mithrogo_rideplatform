Drive with MithroGo

Two simple ways to pay. Pick the one that suits you.

Choose your plan

Plan A – ₹30 a day. Pay a small fixed fee for each day you want to take rides. Everything you earn from fares is yours. Current prices are always shown in the captain app.

Plan B – 1 ride a day. No daily fee. Give the fare of just 1 of your first 5 rides each day. Choose which of your first 4 rides to give, or the 5th is taken automatically. Every other ride that day is yours, even if you do 100. If a day ends with fewer than 5 rides and you haven’t given one, you pay ₹30 and your first ride the next day is taken.

Frequently asked questions

 

Which cities are you in?

We are launching in one city first. More cities will follow once the service is running well.

What vehicles are available?

Bike and Auto rides are available at launch. More ride types will be added later.

How do I pay for a ride?

You see the fare before you book, and that fare does not change during the trip. Pay online in the app, or in cash to your captain when the ride is booked as a cash ride.

Can I cancel a ride?

Yes, you can cancel from the app until the ride starts.

How much does a captain pay?

Two ways: ₹30 a day, or give the fare of just 1 ride a day. With the second plan you choose which of your first 4 rides to give; if you don't, the 5th ride is taken automatically. Every other ride that day is yours. If you finish a day with fewer than 5 rides and haven't given one, the next day you pay ₹30 and your first ride is taken.

How do I report a problem?

Open the ride in the app and choose Help, or contact us using the email below. Our team reviews every report.
